Our seva in Vraja

Sevayu Foundation is committed to serving the residents of Vraja through food, healthcare, education, and daily support. Each act of service to Vrajavāsīs is an offering to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, bringing joy and dignity to His sacred land.

  • Food

    Prasadam from the food van and monthly ration kits for families.

  • Healthcare

    Medicines and care for widows and elderly Vrajavāsīs.

  • Education

    School kits and support so children can keep learning.

  • Daily support

    Clean drinking water, blankets and help when it is needed.

Non profit

Sevayu Foundation is a non-profit, charitable organisation dedicated solely to the service of society. Every contribution received is directly utilised for welfare projects.

How do I give a seva?

Choose a seva above, or any amount, and press “Send my seva on WhatsApp” on your plate. We reply with our UPI and bank details, and confirm once your seva is received.

Where does my money go?

Sevayu Foundation is a non-profit, charitable organisation. Every contribution received is directly utilised for welfare projects in Vraja: food, healthcare, education and daily support.

Can I give every month?

Yes. Choose “Every month” on your plate of seva and we will remind you each month, so a family can count on rations or a child on school.

Can I give in someone’s name?

Yes. For a birthday, an anniversary or in memory of a loved one, write the name in your WhatsApp message and the seva will be offered in their name.

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will send a receipt for your contribution, with any tax-exemption details that apply.

How can my company partner with you?

We warmly invite corporate partners for CSR projects, from food vans to water points. Call or message us and we will share a proposal.
