Our mission

Selfless service

Sevayu Foundation is dedicated to selfless service, aiming to uplift society through education, healthcare, food distribution, and spiritual welfare. Guided by compassion, we strive to make someone happy by bringing relief, dignity, and hope to those in need.

Our vision

“To nurture a world guided by compassion for all living beings (Jīve Dāya), practicing selfless, heartfelt service (Vaiṣṇava Seva). We aim to bring joy, dignity, and hope to every life we touch, fostering a society where love, empathy, and spiritual values guide every action.”

Core values

  • Compassion (Dāya)Treat every living being with kindness and empathy, recognizing the sanctity of all life.
  • Selfless service (Sevā)Serve others with sincerity and devotion, without expectation of reward.
  • Integrity and transparencyUphold honesty, ethical conduct, and accountability in all actions.
  • Joyful giving (Ananda in Seva)Spread happiness through every act of service, inspiring hope and positivity.
  • Spiritual inspirationBase all initiatives on timeless spiritual principles, fostering inner growth along with social welfare.
  • Non profitSevayu Foundation is a non-profit, charitable organisation dedicated solely to the service of society. Every contribution received is directly utilised for welfare projects.

Our activities

Sevayu Foundation is committed to serving the residents of Vraja through food, healthcare, education, and daily support. Each act of service to Vrajavāsīs is an offering to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, bringing joy and dignity to His sacred land.

01

Old age home

Sevayu Foundation facilitates a serene and loving Home for the Aged and Retired in the sacred town of Vrindavan Brajdham, offering elderly individuals a peaceful and spiritually enriched environment. Surrounded by the divine atmosphere of Lord Krishna’s land, the foundation provides not just physical care, but also emotional and spiritual support. Residents are offered comfortable accommodations, medical attention, and the opportunity to engage in daily prayers, bhajans, and satsangs, helping them live their golden years with dignity, devotion, and inner peace.

Give to this seva

02

Rehabilitation home

Sevayu Foundation facilitates a dedicated Rehabilitation Home in Vrindavan & Brajdham for the blind, those affected by leprosy, and individuals with mental disabilities. Rooted in compassion and service, the foundation provides shelter, medical care, and emotional support to some of the most vulnerable members of society. In the spiritually uplifting atmosphere of Brajdham, residents are treated with dignity and love, helping them regain a sense of belonging and purpose. Sevayu Foundation’s mission is to ensure that no one is forgotten, offering hope and healing in the divine land of Lord Krishna.

Give to this seva

03

Widow care

Sevayu Foundation facilitates a compassionate Home for Widows in the holy town of Vrindavan Brajdham, offering shelter, care, and dignity to women who have been abandoned or left alone. In a place revered for its spiritual significance, the foundation provides a safe and peaceful environment where widows can find solace, community, and purpose. Along with basic necessities and healthcare, the home encourages participation in devotional activities like bhajans, kirtans, and satsangs, helping the women heal emotionally and spiritually while living their remaining years with respect and inner peace.

Give to this seva

04

Green and clean Vrindavan

Sevayu Foundation is dedicated to keeping Vrindavan and Brajdham green, clean, and spiritually vibrant. Through tree planting and maintenance, cleanliness drives across streets, kundas, and ghats, and environmental awareness programs, we engage the community and devotees in preserving the sanctity of this holy land. By promoting sustainable practices, Sevayu Foundation strives to maintain Vrindavan as a serene and pure place for devotion and spiritual growth, honoring its legacy as the beloved land of Lord Krishna.

Give to this seva

05

Gosevā & Ahimsā milk

At Sevayu Foundation, we honor the sacred tradition of Gosevā: caring for cows and bulls with love and devotion, including those living on the streets. Our Ahimsā Milk initiative provides pure, cruelty-free milk, ensuring the welfare of all bovines while offering nourishing, ethical products to society. Through this, we embody compassion for all living beings (Jīve Dāya) and the spirit of selfless service to all jīvas (Jīva Seva).

Give to this seva

06

Prasadam distribution

Sevayu Foundation strives to nourish both body and soul. Through our Prasadam distribution, we share the sanctified joy of devotional food with communities, spreading love and spiritual connection. Alongside this, our monthly groceries program provides essential provisions to underprivileged families, ensuring sustenance, dignity, and hope in their daily lives. Every act reflects our commitment to compassion (Jīve Dāya) and selfless Vaishnava service (Vaishnava Seva).

Give to this seva

07

Support during calamities

Sevayu Foundation stands ready to support communities during natural challenges, providing relief and care during floods, extreme summers, and harsh winters. Through timely distribution of food, water, clothing, and emergency aid, we ensure the safety and well-being of affected families. Our efforts reflect our commitment to compassion (Jīve Dāya) and selfless service in times of crisis.

Give to this seva

08

Medical assistance

Sevayu Foundation provides basic medical assistance to the poor and needy with the generous support of devoted doctors and hospitals. From routine health check-ups to essential medicines and care, we strive to ensure that everyone has access to proper healthcare, reflecting our commitment to compassion (Jīve Dāya) and selfless service (Vaishnava Seva).

Give to this seva

09

Safe drinking water

Sevayu Foundation ensures safe and pure drinking water for the residents of Vrindavan, addressing the issue of naturally salty water in the region. Through water purification and distribution efforts, we provide access to clean, healthy water, reflecting our commitment to compassion and selfless service.

How do I give a seva?

Choose a seva above, or any amount, and press “Send my seva on WhatsApp” on your plate. We reply with our UPI and bank details, and confirm once your seva is received.

Where does my money go?

Sevayu Foundation is a non-profit, charitable organisation. Every contribution received is directly utilised for its welfare activities in Vrindavan Brajdham: the old age home, the rehabilitation home, widow care, gau seva, prasadam and monthly groceries, medical assistance, safe drinking water, a green and clean Vrindavan, and relief during calamities.

Can I give every month?

Yes. Choose “Every month” on your plate of seva and we will remind you each month, so an elder, a widow or a cow can count on your seva month after month.

Can I give in someone’s name?

Yes. For a birthday, an anniversary or in memory of a loved one, write the name in your WhatsApp message and the seva will be offered in their name.

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will send a receipt for your contribution, with any tax-exemption details that apply.

How can my company partner with you?

We warmly invite corporate partners for CSR projects, from the old age home and gau seva to safe drinking water and a green and clean Vrindavan. Call or message us and we will share a proposal.
